About satellite imagery and limitations

The standard map opens first for reliability. Tap Satellite imagery to request NASA imagery. Select a date to compare satellite imagery. Cloud cover and black satellite swath gaps can occur. Mostly dark tiles are hidden where browser image inspection permits; edge-connected near-black pixels in other tiles are made transparent when supported. Some gaps can remain. Dark swaths are NOT evidence of haze. Use Try alternate satellite or Previous day if coverage is poor. Use the visibility slider to compare satellite imagery with the standard map beneath. Missing satellite coverage may still appear as dark or rectangular swaths. Try an earlier date if Singapore is obscured. The date is requested, not independently verified as a successful satellite observation.

Why is the hotspot overlay unavailable?

The experimental NASA GIBS WMS overlay is temporarily disabled because direct image requests returned HTTP 504 and no tiles loaded on Android. This does not indicate that there are no fires. Hotspots are satellite thermal anomalies, not confirmed fires.
