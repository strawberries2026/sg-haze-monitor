🛡️ Find or collect N95 masks

Check whether an official mask collection exercise is active, or find pharmacies where you can purchase N95 respirators.

🎁 Check government mask collection

If an official distribution exercise is active, use the government locator to check participating Community Clubs (CCs) or vending machines, eligibility and availability.

Collection schemes may be temporary or inactive. Check the official site for current availability and eligibility before travelling.

↗ Check official mask collection locator

Enter your six-digit postal code on the official site, if its locator is currently available. No postal code is collected by this dashboard.

Who can collect?

Eligibility depends on the current government distribution exercise. Historical eligibility rules may no longer apply. Confirm requirements with the official organiser.

🛒 Buy N95 masks

For residents who prefer to purchase masks, browse nearby pharmacies. Retailer listings do not guarantee N95 stock.

Find pharmacies selling N95 masks ↗

Choosing a mask

A properly fitted N95 respirator filters fine particles better than a loose-fitting surgical mask. A good face seal matters. N95 masks are generally not required for short outdoor exposure; follow official haze advice based on exposure and health needs.

People with heart or lung conditions should seek medical advice before wearing a tight-fitting respirator. Adult N95 masks may not fit children properly.
